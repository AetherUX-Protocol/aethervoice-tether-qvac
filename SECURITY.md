Security Policy: AetherVoice Sovereign Intelligence
Our Commitment to Privacy
At AetherVoice, security is not a feature; it is the foundation of our existence. Our project is built on the "Sovereign Intelligence" philosophy: sensitive B2B trade data (refinery mandates, broker communications, and invoices) must never leave the user’s local environment.

Silicon-Level Privacy
AetherVoice leverages the Tether QVAC SDK to perform 100% of AI inference locally. By design, our application does not:

Route transactional data to centralized cloud APIs.

Store unencrypted deal metadata on external servers.

Train global models on your private trade secrets.

Compliance with 2026 Standards
AetherVoice is engineered to support the rigorous security and evidence requirements of:

Digital Trade Facilitation Bill (DTFB) 2026: Maintaining the 'Reliable Method' for document integrity.

Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2026: Ensuring local certification of digital records as Primary Evidence.

Reporting a Vulnerability
We take all security reports seriously. If you discover a vulnerability in our local inference engine, the Tether WDK bridge, or our UI, please follow these steps:

Do Not Open a Public Issue: To protect our users and the integrity of the B2B trades being audited, we ask that you report bugs privately.

Contact Us: Please email your findings to [Your Professional Email Address].

Submission Format:

Description of the vulnerability.

Steps to reproduce.

Potential impact on the local QVAC environment or Solana WDK wallet.

Scope
This policy applies to the AetherVoice source code and its specific integration of the QVAC SDK and Tether WDK. For vulnerabilities discovered within the underlying SDKs themselves, we will coordinate directly with the Tether and Holepunch security teams.

Response Timeline
Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours.

Initial Evaluation: Within 72 hours.

Resolution/Patch: Timelines will vary based on the complexity of the fix, but we prioritize issues affecting local data isolation.
